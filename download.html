System Requirements

Mac

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel — one universal app. Built natively for SwiftUI, SwiftData, and PDFKit.

iPhone & iPad

iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Apple Pencil is supported on iPad for freehand ink and annotation workflows.

Apple Intelligence

Key Findings, Explain Connections, Explain Figure, and concept tagging need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. They run on the device with Apple's on-device model; everything else in Papyrus works without them.

Pricing

  • Papyrus is free to download and use, with every feature included.
  • No in-app purchases, no recurring subscription, and no Papyrus account required.

What's Included

  • Local-first research library with PDFs, metadata, tags (with Top Tags and a searchable All Tags list), folders (a paper can live in several), colored flags, Smart Collections, and Projects.
  • Zotero and Mendeley import: bring an exported library across with each paper's PDF and tags, filed in its own From Zotero or From Mendeley folder.
  • Safari import extension: capture the paper on the open tab — including papers behind a publisher sign-in you already have access to — or save any PDF, DOI, or arXiv link, with folder, tag, and project pickers right in the popup.
  • Native PDF reading with highlights, underlines, strikethrough, notes, bookmarks, and freehand ink, plus a Reading card that remembers your page and your bookmarks.
  • Markdown notes with LaTeX math and code rendering, readable in full from a paper's note card.
  • Similar Papers: related papers already in your library, ranked on the device with a reason for each, plus optional OpenAlex suggestions that stay off until you turn them on.
  • On-device Apple Intelligence, when you ask: a Key Findings card with an overview and three to five findings, Explain Connections for Similar Papers, and Explain Figure in the reader, all streamed as they are written.
  • Notebooks: gather notes from across the library onto themed shelves without moving them off their papers, then search, reorder, and export a shelf as one Markdown document.
  • Project Evidence Board with claims, quotes, page numbers, citation keys, roles, and confidence.
  • Full-text search across library metadata, notes, annotations, and extracted PDF text.
  • Optional iCloud Sync across Mac, iPad, and iPhone, plus WebDAV sync with restore points.
  • Export to BibTeX, RIS, CSL JSON, Markdown, copied citations, and project bundles — and papyrus:// links to any paper or notebook for Hookmark and other link managers.
