A native, local-first research desk for importing papers, reading PDFs, writing notes, building project evidence, syncing with your own WebDAV server, and exporting open citation formats.

System Requirements

Mac

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Built natively for SwiftUI, SwiftData, and PDFKit.

iPad

iPadOS 17 or later. Apple Pencil is supported for freehand ink and annotation workflows.

Trial & Pricing

  • 30-day full-access trial for each edition.
  • Mac App Store lifetime unlock: US $3.99.
  • App Store for iPad lifetime unlock: US $5.99.
  • No recurring subscription and no Papyrus account.
  • Final regional pricing, taxes, refunds, purchase records, and Family Sharing behavior are handled by Apple.

What's Included

  • Local-first research library with PDFs, metadata, tags, folders, Smart Collections, and Projects.
  • Native PDF reading with highlights, underlines, strikethrough, notes, bookmarks, and freehand ink.
  • Markdown notes with LaTeX math and code rendering.
  • Project Evidence Board with claims, quotes, page numbers, citation keys, roles, and confidence.
  • Full-text search across library metadata, notes, annotations, and extracted PDF text.
  • Optional WebDAV sync with restore points.
  • Export to BibTeX, RIS, CSL JSON, Markdown, copied citations, and project bundles.
