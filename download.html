What's included

Everything in one app.
Nothing held back.

Native PDF reader

Highlights, underlines, comments, ink annotations, bookmarks, and reading progress — built on PDFKit.

Evidence Board

Capture quotes, page numbers, claims, themes, interpretations, sections, citation keys, and confidence inside a project.

Project synthesis

Project-specific roles, theme maps, scoped search, note templates, and manuscript preflight checks.

WebDAV sync

Optional. Bring your own server — Nextcloud, Synology, Fastmail, local NAS, or any standard WebDAV endpoint.

Restore points

Successful WebDAV syncs keep dated restore points so you can inspect and restore an earlier library copy.

Open exports

BibTeX, RIS, CSL JSON, Markdown, evidence, cite commands, and full project bundles.
