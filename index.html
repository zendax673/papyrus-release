One loop, one app

From a stack of PDFs
to a cited argument.

Station 01

Import & read

Drop a PDF, paste a DOI, drag a folder. Read in a native window and mark it up — highlight, underline, ink, and notes. ⌘O ⌘F

Station 02

Capture a claim

Send a quote into the Evidence Board with its paper, page, and section role — Claim, Method, Limitation, and more.

Station 03

Set confidence

Rate how strongly the source supports the claim and add your own interpretation before you draft.

Station 04

Export the argument

Three rules of the desk

Built to fit your research,
not to own it.

Most reference tools want to become your workflow and hold your library hostage in a web account. Papyrus Papers is a desk, not a destination.

Local-first, always

Your library, PDFs, notes, and indexes live on your Mac and iPad. No Papyrus account. The network is touched only when you choose metadata lookup or WebDAV sync.

Yours to verify

Every claim keeps its quote, page, citation key, and a confidence you set. Per-field provenance shows where metadata came from when sources disagree.

Every format, open

In: PDF, DOI, arXiv, BibTeX, RIS, folders. Out: BibTeX, RIS, CSL JSON, Markdown, and project bundles. Files stay portable; the workflow stays yours.

Read & think

PDF and notes,
side by side.

A native PDFKit reader paired with Markdown notes that understand LaTeX — reading and writing in one window, not two apps.

  • Highlight, underline, strikethrough, freehand ink, notes, and bookmarks
  • Markdown notes with LaTeX math and code, right beside the page
  • Full-text search across the entire library
  • A reading queue and projects keep work-in-progress visible across sessions
Write from evidence

A project becomes
an argument.

The Evidence Board turns scattered highlights into a structured case — every claim carries its source, page, citation key, and your confidence.

  • Capture claim, quote, interpretation, page, citation key, and a 1–5 confidence
  • Group evidence by Claim, Related Work, Method, Result, Limitation, Future Work, or Rebuttal
  • Project-specific paper roles — To Skim, Deep Read, Cite in Method, Background, Excluded, Needs Re-check
  • Scoped synthesis search across the project's metadata, notes, highlights, and PDF text
Export

Out the way
you came in.

Everything you read and organized flows into the formats your writing tools expect — one command, no reformatting.

  • Copy citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, Harvard, Vancouver, BibTeX, and RIS
  • Project-level BibTeX, RIS, CSL JSON, Markdown, and full bundle export
  • Copy cite commands for LaTeX and Pandoc Markdown workflows
  • An export report flags missing PDFs, duplicate citekeys, and missing DOI / year / pages
